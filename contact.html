FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can be requested and are subject to availability.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes — airport pickup and drop-off can be arranged in advance. Please mention it in your special requests when booking or contact us directly.

Is parking available on-site?

All guests have access to free, secure and gated parking within the lodge grounds.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes — contact us with your booking reference at least 48 hours before check-in and we'll help you amend or cancel your reservation.

Do you accommodate families and large groups?

Our Family Room sleeps up to five guests across two bedrooms. For larger groups, contact us directly and we'll help coordinate multiple rooms.
