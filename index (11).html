Welcome

Lusaka living, reimagined.

Tucked into the leafy streets of Kabulonga, Urban Haven Lodge blends the calm of a residential neighbourhood with the polish of a modern city hotel. Every room is designed around clean lines, deep-green textures and warm gold accents — a space that feels considered, not corporate.

Whether you're in town for a week of meetings, a family visit, or simply passing through Lusaka, we built this lodge to be the reliable, elegant base your stay deserves — at a rate that respects your budget.
