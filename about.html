About Us

Built for the way people actually travel to Lusaka.

Our Story

A lodge built around Kabulonga's quiet character.

Urban Haven Lodge started with a simple observation: Lusaka had plenty of large hotels and plenty of budget guesthouses, but very little in between — a place with genuinely thoughtful design, reliable service, and a fair price.

We chose Kabulonga for its tree-lined streets and easy access to the rest of the city, and designed every room around comfort first: good beds, fast Wi-Fi, quiet air conditioning, and staff who remember your name by day two.

Our Mission

To give every guest — whether in Lusaka for one night or one month — a comfortable, reliable and genuinely welcoming place to stay, without the inflated price tag of a big-box hotel.

Our Vision

To be Lusaka's most trusted independent lodge — known by business travellers, families and tourists alike as the address that feels like a genuine haven in the city.

Our Values

Warmth, attention to detail, and honesty in pricing. What you see when you book is what you pay when you arrive.
